## Email Best Practices

### Subject Lines
- Keep under 50 characters
- Use numbers when possible
- Create urgency (but don't overdo it)
- Personalize when appropriate
- Test different variations

### Content
- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences)
- Use bullet points
- Include clear CTAs
- Mobile-friendly formatting
- Personalize with [Name]

### Timing
- Welcome series: Day 0, 3, 7
- Newsletter: First Tuesday of month
- Re-engagement: 30 days inactive
- Feature announcements: As needed

### Segmentation
- New users: Welcome series
- Active users: Feature updates
- Inactive users: Re-engagement
- Customers: Success stories, tips
- Free users: Upgrade prompts (gentle)

### A/B Testing Ideas
- Subject lines
- CTA button text
- Email length (short vs detailed)
- Personalization level
- Send time
