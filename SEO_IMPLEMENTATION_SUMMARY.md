## 📝 Content Creation Workflow

### For Each Blog Post:
1. **Research** (1-2 hours)
   - Check competitor content
   - Find authoritative sources
   - Identify internal linking opportunities

2. **Write** (3-4 hours)
   - Follow template outline
   - Include target keywords naturally
   - Add internal/external links
   - Include CTAs

3. **Optimize** (1 hour)
   - Add meta description
   - Optimize images (alt tags)
   - Check keyword density
   - Add schema markup

4. **Publish** (30 min)
   - Upload to website
   - Update sitemap
   - Submit to Google Search Console
   - Share on social media

5. **Promote** (Ongoing)
   - LinkedIn post
   - Twitter/X posts

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Content Quality > Quantity**: Better to publish 1 great post per month than 4 mediocre ones

2. **Internal Linking**: Link new content to existing pages (and vice versa) to boost SEO

3. **Update Old Content**: Refresh existing pages with new keywords and information

4. **User Intent**: Focus on what users are actually searching for, not just keywords

5. **Mobile First**: Ensure all new pages are mobile-optimized (already good with Tailwind)

6. **Page Speed**: Already excellent (browser-based), but monitor as content grows

7. **Social Proof**: Add customer testimonials and case studies as you get them

8. **Consistency**: Stick to the content calendar for best results
